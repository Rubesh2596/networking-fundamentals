# Lab 02: Understand IP addresses

## Difficulty
Beginner

## Estimated completion time
20 minutes

## Learning objectives
- Understand private and public addressing
- Check your address and subnet
- Explain the role of a gateway

## Commands
```bash
ip addr
ip route
```

## Questions
1. Is your interface configured with a private or public address?
2. What subnet does it belong to?
3. What route allows traffic to leave the local network?
