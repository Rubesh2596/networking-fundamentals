# Lab 06: Inspect local listening ports

## Difficulty
Beginner

## Estimated completion time
20 minutes

## Learning objectives
- Understand listening ports
- Learn how to inspect local services
- Distinguish a local listener from public exposure

## Commands
```bash
ss -tulpn
netstat -ano
```

## Questions
1. Which ports are listening?
2. Which service is bound to each port?
3. Is the service visible outside the local machine?
