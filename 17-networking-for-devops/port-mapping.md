# Port Mapping

## Overview

This topic is especially relevant for developers, DevOps engineers, and anyone troubleshooting modern systems.

## Why it matters

Modern services often depend on local ports, load balancers, firewalls, containers, and proxy layers. These add real-world complexity beyond the textbook model.

## Workflow

1. Identify the service
2. Check the route or port
3. Observe traffic or logs
4. Confirm the fix
